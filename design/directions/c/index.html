AVGVR

An augur once read the future from the flight of birds, and here the augur is a legion war machine with one pink sensor eye. Olive armor plate, bronze trim gone green at the edges and Latin capitals hold a wide and quiet field. The eye is the only thing that moves: it sweeps the horizon while your agents work, and it stops on the one thing that needs you.

I

MARK

An original spread-wing eagle above two open laurel branches, drawn in flat bronze plates with dark cut lines. There is no disc behind it. The eagle looks right, toward the good omen. The pink eye is the only accent color in the mark. It replaces the helmet mark of revision 1.

File: mark.svg, square viewBox. Use it at 22 px and larger. Keep clear space of 1/8 of its width on each side.

IX

BUILD NOTES

What is hard to build, by part. Levels: easy, medium, hard.

N1SDDM

mediumNeeds a hand-written Main.qml. The full-width slit, the 2-button session picker (a Repeater over sessionModel) and the eye (a NumberAnimation on x) are simple QML. The Latin date needs a small JS function for Roman numerals and day names.

N2SDDM

hardAn Omarchy theme switch does not retint the greeter. The colors must be written into theme.conf by a script that runs with sudo, and the fonts must be installed system-wide or loaded with FontLoader from the theme folder.

N3PLYMOUTH

hardUse the script plugin. The eye, the trail plate and the numerals must be pre-rendered PNG sprites, because plymouth has no blur and limited text. Drive the trail from Plymouth.SetBootProgressFunction. The disk password prompt must also fit in the slit. Ship sprites at 2× for 5120×2160.

N4PLYMOUTH

mediumAny font used in the splash must be in the initramfs. Rebuilding the theme and the initramfs is a separate sudo step.

N5LOCK

mediumhyprlock can draw the slit and rails with shape widgets and put an input-field in the slit. It cannot animate the slit opening or the eye sweep, so the lock stays static. The fleet line is a label with cmd[update:2000] that calls the horch status script.

N6BAR

mediumRoman workspaces are a format-icons map in waybar. The fleet counter is a custom module that reads horch status as JSON and sets a waiting class. The sweeping eye is possible with GTK CSS keyframes but fragile. Quickshell (QML) makes the eye easy and is the better fit if the bar is rebuilt.

N7WALLPAPER

easyhyprpaper and SDDM need a PNG. rsvg-convert ignores the embedded fonts, so export with Chrome: google-chrome-stable --headless --screenshot=wallpaper.png --window-size=5120,2160 wallpaper.svg, or convert the text to paths first.

N8HYPRLAND

easyWindow look is plain config: rounding = 0, bronze col.active_border, muted inactive border, no title bars. The ghostty and Claude Code colors come from colors.toml through the normal Omarchy templates.
