An augur read the future in the flight of birds. Here the augur watches your agents. Warm stone, bronze, one porphyry stripe and carved Latin capitals hold a wide and quiet field, under a crested Roman helmet in open laurel. IX

BUILD NOTES

What is hard to build, by part. Levels: easy, medium, hard.

N1SDDM

mediumNeeds a hand-written Main.qml. The full-width band, the dentil rails, the 2-button session picker (a Repeater over sessionModel) and the bird (an SVG Image with a NumberAnimation on x) are simple QML. The Latin date needs a small JS function for Roman numerals and day names.

N2SDDM

hardAn Omarchy theme switch does not retint the greeter. The colors must be written into theme.conf by a script that runs with sudo, and the fonts must be installed system-wide or loaded with FontLoader from the theme folder.

N3PLYMOUTH

hardUse the script plugin. The mark, the bird, the dentil block and the numerals must be pre-rendered PNG sprites, because plymouth has limited vector and text support. Drive the dentil row from Plymouth.SetBootProgressFunction. The disk password prompt must also fit in the band. Ship sprites at 2× for 5120×2160.

N4PLYMOUTH

mediumAny font used in the splash must be in the initramfs. Rebuilding the theme and the initramfs is a separate sudo step.

N5LOCK

mediumhyprlock can draw the band and the dentil rails with shape widgets, the mark and the perched bird with image widgets, and put an input-field in the band. It cannot animate, so the lock stays static. The fleet line is a label with cmd[update:2000] that calls the horch status script.

N6BAR

mediumRoman workspaces are a format-icons map in waybar. The brand mark is an image module at 32 px. The fleet counter is a custom module that reads horch status as JSON and sets a waiting class. The flying bird is possible with GTK CSS keyframes on a background image but fragile. Quickshell (QML) makes the bird easy and is the better fit if the bar is rebuilt.

N7WALLPAPER

easyhyprpaper and SDDM need a PNG. rsvg-convert ignores the embedded font, so export with Chrome: google-chrome-stable --headless --screenshot=wallpaper.png --window-size=5120,2160 wallpaper.svg, or convert the text to paths first.

N8HYPRLAND

easyWindow look is plain config: rounding = 0, bronze col.active_border, border-colored inactive border, no title bars. The ghostty and Claude Code colors come from colors.toml through the normal Omarchy templates.
